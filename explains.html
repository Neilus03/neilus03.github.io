Gaussian splatting,
in five minutes.

A scene is not stored as a pile of pixels. It is a cloud of soft, coloured 3D ellipses that blend into an image.

01 / 03 Many tiny blobs,
one coherent view. Move your cursor through the scene
01

Capture

Images from many viewpoints reveal where visual evidence lives in 3D.

02

Represent

Each Gaussian has a position, scale, colour, opacity, and orientation.

03

Splat

Project and blend the ellipses. New views appear at real-time speed.

∴

The trick: approximate geometry with a dense, learnable cloud of translucent ellipses—fast enough to make novel-view synthesis feel immediate.
